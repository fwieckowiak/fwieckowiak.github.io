This paper introduces **PatentME**, a dataset and reference-free post-OCR verification task for printed mathematical expression recognition. The work addresses the difficulty of evaluating mathematical expression recognition systems from scanned patent documents, where noisy inputs, multiple font styles, and missing reference markup make evaluation incomplete.

The proposed benchmark includes two datasets:

- **PatentME-OCR**, with roughly 41k images of mathematical expressions extracted from patent documents and paired with MathML annotations.
- **PatentME-Siamese**, built from OCR erroneous predictions and designed for a post-OCR verification task where a judge model determines whether two rendered expressions are semantically equivalent.
